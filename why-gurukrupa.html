One Integrated Service

Concept to finished piece under one roof.

With over three decades of manufacturing experience, Gurukrupa Export delivers complete jewellery solutions for brands, retailers and businesses. From concept and design to development, production and finishing, our flexible approach helps partners achieve quality, consistency and efficiency across their jewellery programmes.

Crafted to Perfection

100% natural diamonds. BIS-hallmarked gold.

Our artisans bring decades of expertise to transform raw materials into timeless works of art. Multi-stage processes from design sketch to final polish guarantee every piece meets our signature standards.

Innovation Meets Tradition

Masters of close-setting.

We specialise in the intricate art of close-setting, maximising diamond brilliance and durability. From CAD modelling to precise setting and quality certification, heritage and modern engineering work seamlessly together.
