About Gurukrupa Export

A legacy of craftsmanship. A vision for tomorrow.

Gurukrupa — “the grace of the teacher”. Since 1962, three generations of the Ramani family have carried that grace from the master karigar to the apprentice, and from our workshop to your showroom.

We deliver end-to-end jewellery manufacturing solutions tailored to brands and jewellery businesses. From design development and CAD to precision manufacturing, finishing and quality assurance, we support every stage of the journey — helping our partners bring their ideas to market with confidence.

The Journey of a Piece

From sketch to showcase.

Every Gurukrupa piece passes through six stages under one roof — design freedom, consistent quality and dependable timelines.

  1. 01

    Inspiration & Sketch

    Heritage, culture and global trends become hand sketches.

  2. 02

    CAD & Visualisation

    Advanced CAD models, renders and production-ready files.

  3. 03

    Casting

    BIS-hallmarked gold, cast in-house for consistency.

  4. 04

    Close-Setting

    Our speciality — maximising diamond brilliance and durability.

  5. 05

    Quality Check

    Multi-stage inspection of stones, metal and finish.

  6. 06

    Delivery

    Certified, tracked and delivered securely on time.
