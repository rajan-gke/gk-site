Our Story

Made with kripa.

Since 1962, Gurukrupa Export has been a pioneer in diamond sourcing and jewellery craftsmanship, led by Shri Nagjibhai Ramani’s visionary journey. In 1997 the Ramani family transformed tradition with the establishment of Gurukrupa, driving excellence in diamond jewellery manufacturing.

Over the decades, branch expansions across India strengthened our presence, while landmark temple jewellery commissions showcased our cultural artistry. ISO accreditations and national recognition reflect a legacy built on enduring values and ethical practices that continue to inspire.
